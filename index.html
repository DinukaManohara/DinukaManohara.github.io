PhD Candidate & Software / Research Engineer

I'm a PhD candidate at the University of Melbourne working on automated data exploration, with a background spanning graph mining research, IoT platforms, and full-stack software engineering across Sri Lanka, the UK, and Singapore. This is my little corner of the internet — where I write about what I'm working on and share things I've made.

Background

Where I come from

I grew up in Ambalangoda, Sri Lanka, and studied Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Moratuwa, graduating with First Class Honours. From there I worked as a software and R&D engineer across Sri Lanka, the UK, and Singapore — building IoT platforms, mobile apps, and machine learning pipelines — before moving to Melbourne to pursue a PhD.

What I do

My current focus

I'm currently a PhD candidate at the University of Melbourne, working on automated data exploration — discovering insights through intent prediction and guided exploration. Before this, I worked on large-scale graph data mining, and led engineering on IoT platforms for disaster risk management. See my Publications and Projects pages for the details.

Beyond work

Outside the screen

Outside of research, I swam competitively for my university and hold a certificate in Eastern Classical Music (violin). I also have a soft spot for entrepreneurship — I've served on startup committees, mentored student teams, and co-founded a startup of my own along the way.
